# Activity Time – Project Overview

## 1. Project Introduction

**Activity Time** is an online activity booking and management platform designed for activity providers, coaches, teachers, and businesses that organise children's camps, classes, workshops, and other activity-based programs.

The platform simplifies the process of creating activity listings, managing bookings, accepting payments, handling registrations, and managing customer information.

It provides a centralised solution where activity providers can manage their operations while parents can discover activities, book available spaces, and complete payments online.

**Official Website:** https://www.activity-time.com/

**Project Type:** Activity Booking & Management Platform  
**Primary Market:** Ireland  
**Primary Users:** Activity Providers and Parents / Customers

---

## 2. What Does Activity Time Do?

Activity Time helps activity providers manage their business operations through an integrated booking and administration system.

### A. Activity Listing Management

Providers can:

- Create and publish activity listings.
- Add activity descriptions, photos, dates, timings, locations, and pricing.
- Edit, duplicate, snooze, delete, and share listings.
- Manage multiple activities through a centralised dashboard.
- Create bookable activity pages without requiring their own website.

### B. Online Booking & Payments

The platform allows parents and customers to:

- Browse available activities.
- Book activity spaces online.
- Make payments through supported payment options.
- Manage booking-related information.
- Receive automatic booking confirmations and reminders.

The platform also supports deposits, balance payments, and flexible fee options.

### C. Booking & Registration Management

Providers can manage:

- Customer bookings and registrations.
- Real-time booking information.
- Attendee lists and customer details.
- Booking confirmations and reminders.
- Waiting lists.
- Printable sign-in sheets.
- Photo permissions, waivers, and special notes.

### D. Scheduling & Pricing Management

Activity Time supports several booking and pricing capabilities, including:

- Multi-day activity scheduling.
- Early-bird discounts.
- Sibling discounts.
- Early and late pick-up options.
- Trial spaces.
- Waiting lists.
- Deposit and balance management.

### E. Provider Administration

The platform provides tools to help providers manage their business operations, including:

- Centralised activity management.
- Customer information management.
- Revenue calculation.
- Automated communication.
- Customer reviews.
- AI-assisted listing creation and content improvement.

### F. Website & Shopfront Integration

Providers can:

- Use their own branded Activity Time shopfront.
- Share booking links through WhatsApp, email, and social media.
- Embed activity booking functionality into an existing website.
- Manage activity listings without needing a separate website.

---

## 3. What Activity Time Does Not Do

Activity Time is a specialised activity booking and administration solution rather than a general-purpose social networking or online shopping platform.

Its primary purpose is to help providers organise and manage activity-based businesses.

Some important distinctions:

- It does not provide the actual camps, classes, or workshops; these are organised and delivered by independent activity providers.
- It does not require every provider to own a separate website.
- Refunds are handled directly between the activity provider and the activity buyer according to the applicable refund policy, rather than being processed as a refund service by Activity Time.
- It is not designed to replace the provider's actual activity operations or on-site service delivery.

---

## 4. Primary User Roles

### Activity Provider

The provider is responsible for creating and managing activities, handling bookings, maintaining customer information, and managing activity-related operations.

### Parent / Customer

Parents and customers can discover activities, view listing details, book available spaces, and make payments.

These two user roles interact through the platform's activity listing and booking workflows.
