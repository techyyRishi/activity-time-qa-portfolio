## 6. Defect Reporting Approach

My defect reporting approach focuses on:

- **Bug Title:** Clear identification of the issue.
- **Description:** Explanation of the observed behavior.
- **Steps to Reproduce:** Structured steps to reproduce the issue.
- **Priority:** Indication of the issue's urgency for resolution.
- **Evidence:** Supporting screenshots and relevant references.
